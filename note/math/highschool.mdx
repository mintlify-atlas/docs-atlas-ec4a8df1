High school mathematics in China is structured around two tracks of textbooks: 必修 (required) volumes studied by all students, and 选择性必修 (elective) volumes taken by students who choose mathematics as a gaokao subject. These notes follow the **人教版 A版** (PEP Edition A), which is standard in Zhejiang and most provinces — two required volumes and three elective volumes totaling five books of progressively deeper content.

Most of this material was first encountered during middle school out of personal curiosity. The official textbook definitions are rigorous but can be opaque; supplementing them with video explanations from multiple perspectives makes a real difference. These notes aim to be both precise and intuitive.

<Note>
Content marked with an asterisk (`*`) in the official textbook is optional enrichment material and is **not tested on the gaokao**.
</Note>

## Curriculum Overview

The 人教版 A版 spans five volumes organized as follows:

| Volume | Content Area |
|--------|-------------|
| 必修第一册 | Sets, logic, functions, inequalities, exponential & logarithmic functions, trigonometric functions |
| 必修第二册 | Plane vectors, complex numbers, solid geometry, statistics, probability |
| 选择性必修第一册 | Space vectors, analytic geometry (lines, circles, conics) |
| 选择性必修第二册 | Sequences, derivatives and applications |
| 选择性必修第三册 | Counting principles, random variables & distributions, regression analysis |

## Exam Schedule Reference

Students in Zhejiang take school-level assessments (学考) and university entrance exams (高考/选考) on this schedule:

| Term | Exams |
|------|-------|
| 高一下 | History, Geography, Chemistry, Biology 学考 |
| 高二上 | Politics, Physics 学考 |
| 高二下 | Chinese, **Math**, Technology 学考 |
| 高三上 | English 高考兼学考; selected 选考 subjects |
| 高三下 | Chinese, **Math**, English 高考; selected 选考 subjects |

<Tip>
For Zhejiang's 七选三 system, mathematics is a core 高考 subject (not a 选考 subject) — it is tested in both the Math 学考 (高二下) and the final 高考 (高三下).
</Tip>

## Required Volume 1 Contents

### Chapter 1 — Sets & Logic

- 1.1 The concept of a set
- 1.2 Basic relations between sets
- 1.3 Set operations (union, intersection, complement)
- 1.4 Sufficient and necessary conditions
- 1.5 Universal quantifiers and existential quantifiers

### Chapter 2 — Quadratic Functions, Equations & Inequalities

- 2.1 Properties of equality and inequality
- 2.2 The basic (AM-GM) inequality
- 2.3 Quadratic functions, equations, and inequalities

### Chapter 3 — Functions: Concepts & Properties

- 3.1 Function definition and representations
- 3.2 Monotonicity and parity
- 3.3 Power functions
- 3.4 Applications of functions (I)

### Chapter 4 — Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

- 4.1 Exponents and exponential functions
- 4.2 The exponential function and its graph
- 4.3 Logarithms
- 4.4 The logarithmic function and its graph
- 4.5 Applications of functions (II)

### Chapter 5 — Trigonometric Functions

- 5.1 Angles and radian measure
- 5.2 Trigonometric function definitions
- 5.3 Induction formulas
- 5.4 Graphs and properties of trig functions
- 5.5 Trigonometric identities
- 5.6 The function $y = A\sin(\omega x + \varphi)$
- 5.7 Applications

## Required Volume 2 Contents

### Chapter 6 — Plane Vectors

Vectors as directed quantities, linear operations, the basis theorem, coordinate representation, dot product, and geometric applications.

### Chapter 7 — Complex Numbers

Definitions, the four arithmetic operations, and the geometric interpretation on the complex plane.

### Chapter 8 — Solid Geometry

Basic solids, orthographic projections, surface area and volume, and the spatial relationships (parallelism and perpendicularity) between lines and planes.

### Chapters 9–10 — Statistics & Probability

Random sampling methods, using samples to estimate populations, classical probability, and independent events.

## Elective Volume 1 — Analytic Geometry

Space vectors and their applications to solid geometry, then analytic geometry: equations of lines, circles, ellipses, hyperbolas, and parabolas.

## Elective Volume 2 — Sequences & Calculus

Arithmetic and geometric sequences with their summation formulas, followed by an introduction to derivatives: differentiation rules, and using derivatives to analyze function behavior and solve optimization problems.

## Elective Volume 3 — Counting & Probability

Counting principles, permutations and combinations, the Binomial Theorem, conditional probability and the multiplication rule, discrete random variables (including binomial and hypergeometric distributions), and the normal distribution.
