A full Apple ecosystem, chosen for the seamless integration between devices and the quality of the hardware. The lineup covers every use case: a powerful laptop for development and creative work, a tablet for reading and casual use, a phone for daily communication, a watch for health tracking, and multiple headphone options for different scenarios.

Everything is documented here in detail — not just the devices themselves, but the exact system configuration (Dock, Finder Sidebar, Launchpad organization) that makes them work efficiently.

## MacBook Pro (M3 Max)

The primary workhorse for development, writing, and everything that requires a real screen and keyboard.

**Configuration:**
- 16-inch / Silver
- M3 Max chip
- 14-core CPU / 30-core GPU
- 36GB unified memory
- 1TB SSD
- **Accessory:** Incase hard-shell protective case

<Note>
The MacBook Pro's aluminum chassis can warp if dropped, hence the hard-shell case. Screen protectors and keyboard covers are avoided — the gap between the screen (B side) and keyboard (C side) is too small, causing the keyboard to leave marks on the screen.
</Note>

**Technical specs:** [MacBook Pro — Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/117737)

## Apple Watch Series 10

Worn daily for health tracking, notifications, and workouts.

**Configuration:**
- Titanium / Slate color
- 46mm
- **Band:** Slate Milanese Loop

**Technical specs:** [Apple Watch Series 10 — Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/121202)

The Milanese Loop is comfortable for all-day wear and adjusts precisely without discrete holes. The titanium case is significantly lighter than stainless steel while being more durable than aluminum.

## AirPods Pro 2

The primary earphones for daily use — commuting, studying, and calls.

**Configuration:**
- USB-C / H2 chip / U1 chip
- **Accessory:** Incase lanyard

**Technical specs:** [AirPods Pro 2 — Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/111851)

Active noise cancellation is excellent for focused study sessions in noisy environments. Transparency mode handles situations where situational awareness is needed. The H2 chip provides noticeably cleaner noise cancellation and better audio quality than the first generation.

## AirPods Max

Over-ear headphones for extended listening sessions where audio quality is the priority.

**Configuration:**
- USB-C / H1 chip
- **Accessory:** Smart case (ear cushion protector)

**Technical specs:** [AirPods Max — Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/121205)

The soundstage is excellent for music and films. The computational audio processing (Personalized Spatial Audio) creates a genuinely immersive experience. Heavy compared to typical over-ear headphones, but the mesh headband distributes weight well.

## Powerbeats Pro 2

Sport-focused earphones for exercise, worn during workouts where the AirPods Pro's fit might be unreliable under heavy movement.

**Configuration:**
- Speed Black / H2 chip

**Technical specs:** [Powerbeats Pro 2 — Beats](https://www.beatsbydre.com/earbuds/powerbeats-pro-2)

The ear hook design locks the earphones in place during intense movement. The H2 chip provides the same audio processing capability as AirPods Pro 2. Battery life is significantly longer than AirPods Pro.

## Device Ecosystem Notes

The full Apple ecosystem enables several capabilities that cross-device:

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Handoff** | Start a task on iPhone, continue on Mac without any manual transfer |
| **Universal Clipboard** | Copy on iPhone, paste on Mac (and vice versa) |
| **AirDrop** | Instant file transfer between devices over a local connection |
| **iPhone Mirroring** | Control iPhone directly from Mac screen |
| **Sidecar** | Use iPad as a second display or drawing tablet for Mac |
| **AirPlay** | Stream audio and video between devices |
| **Find My** | Locate all devices (and AirTags) from a single interface |

The Batteries widget in Notification Center shows the charge level of all paired Apple devices simultaneously — a useful quick check before leaving home.
